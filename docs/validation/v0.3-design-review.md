## Findings

### R1 — MAJOR — aggregate status precedence overlaps complete absence

**Status: RESOLVED; independently re-reviewed.**

Owner: Product Owner / Solution Architect; QA must add matching aggregate oracles.

The product status contract prioritizes PARTIAL when “lookup or chain coverage is incomplete” before MISSING when “no complete supported trace exists in a nonempty population.” A nonempty population with supported COMPLETE lookups but zero full chains has incomplete chain coverage and no complete supported trace, satisfying both branches. The stated precedence therefore makes aggregate MISSING unreachable under the literal chain-coverage reading. D305 delegates component summaries to this same precedence; acceptance criteria cover individual missing hops and useful shorter paths but do not disambiguate the zero-supported-chain aggregate.

Required resolution: explicitly distinguish unavailable populations/capabilities, incomplete lookup evidence, a fully observed nonempty population with zero complete chains, a mixture of supported and missing chains, and an all-supported population. Preserve individual gaps and shorter-path PARTIAL labels; specify whether an aggregate with only evidenced shorter paths is MISSING or PARTIAL. Record aggregate fixture expectations without weakening negative-evidence safeguards.

Resolution evidence: product specification now defines N as distinct eligible artifacts, F as artifacts with at least one full supported normative chain, and component population as mapped commit/path occurrences. Its ordered cardinality table separates unusable/empty populations (UNAVAILABLE), incomplete required lookups (PARTIAL), supported COMPLETE nonempty populations with F=0 (MISSING), mixed 0<F<N (PARTIAL), and F=N (VERIFIED). Short individual paths retain PARTIAL and per-hop gaps, even when a complete aggregate has zero full chains. Alternate routes cannot inflate F; direct WI_COMMIT intent coverage remains distinct from normative full-chain coverage. D305 mirrors these rules; new ECL-AC-339 specifies all branches and is mapped by traceability. Reviewer read the changed product/design/AC/matrix text and accepted this owning-role correction.
